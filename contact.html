COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you deliver throughout Lebanon?

Yes, delivery arrangements are available. Exact delivery fees and scheduling depend on your location and are confirmed directly through WhatsApp.

Can I try on items before purchasing?

Yes, our three physical branches in Rayak, Douris, and Taalabaya welcome you to try on any size or shoe fitting with our team's guidance.

How can I verify if an item is in stock?

Simply copy the product SKU from the product details page and message it to us on WhatsApp (+961 76 010 841). We will check physical stock immediately.

What payment methods are used?

Standard cash payment on delivery or cash at branch pickup is customary in Lebanon. Live online card payments are not processed on this demo.
