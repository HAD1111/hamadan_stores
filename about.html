Our Story & Values.

A multi-category clothing and footwear destination built on family trust, approachable style, and local service in Lebanon.

FAMILY FASHION SINCE DAY ONE

Built on community,
value, and real life.

Hamadan Stores was founded with a singular purpose: to make quality fashion and footwear accessible to entire families under one welcoming roof. Rather than forcing shoppers to visit multiple specialty boutiques, Hamadan combines everyday essentials and statement trends across Women, Men, Kids, and Footwear.

With three physical branches situated strategically along key highways in the Bekaa region—Rayak / Zahle Highway, Douris / Baalbeck Highway, and Taalabaya Main Road—we remain closely connected to the daily needs of our local community.

Whether you are preparing children for school, refreshing your seasonal work wardrobe, or finding comfortable trainers for daily wear, our stores are committed to honest service and attentive support.
